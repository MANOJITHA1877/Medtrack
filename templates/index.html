🏥 About MedTrack

MedTrack is a hospital asset tracking and management system developed to simplify the way medical equipment is monitored.

The system allows administrators to add, edit and delete equipment, monitor equipment status, track maintenance and maintain maintenance history.

MedTrack helps hospitals reduce equipment loss, improve maintenance planning and keep important asset information organized.
